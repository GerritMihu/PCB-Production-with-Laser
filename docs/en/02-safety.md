# Safety

🇩🇪 [Deutsch](../de/02-sicherheit.md) · [← Overview](../../README.md)

> This page does **not** replace the operating instructions of the school and the manufacturer.

## Laser radiation

- Only operate the machine with the lid/protective cover closed.
- Never bypass safety devices (lid switch, emergency stop).
- Laser class of the machine: see `machines/<machine>/`.

## Smoke and fumes

Ablating copper and scorching FR4 (glass fiber + epoxy) produces **harmful fumes and fine dust**.

- **Always** switch on extraction/filtering before starting the laser.
- Let it run a little after the job before opening the lid.
- **Cutting FR4** with the fiber laser (160 passes) produces a lot of glass-fiber and epoxy dust. Only cut with the extraction running, and clean the work area afterwards.

## Fire safety

- **Never** leave the laser running unattended.
- Keep a CO₂ fire extinguisher at hand.
- Keep the work area free of flammable material.

## Students

- Students operate the machine only after instruction and under supervision.
